# 0045: The FM and LA voices, DX7 SysEx in Rust, and tables we generate

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-10-17 · **Extends:** 0009, 0011

## Context

Epic #78 brought seven polyphonic instruments; four are subtractive and run on the Mono voice (ADR-0011). Three are not: the PPG Wave plays wavetables (#87), the Roland D-50 splices sampled attacks onto synthesised partials (#88), and the Yamaha DX7 is six-operator FM (#89). #86 asked for a table oscillator shared by the PPG and the D-50, and #90 for loading DX7 voices from `.syx` files. All of it landed in PR #116.

Two constraints shaped it. The instruments' sounds live in ROMs and in other people's code: the DX7's best-known open emulation, Dexed, is GPL-3, while this project is Apache-2.0. And ADR-0002: nothing may allocate or panic in `render`.

The record disagrees with itself on where these voices run. ADR-0011 says the table oscillator and the FM voice are "further `PolyVoice` variants"; ADR-0032 says the table oscillator "runs inside the Mono voice, through its ladder or SVF". Each is half right.

## Decision

**The DX7 and the D-50 are voices of their own (`PolyVoice::Fm`, `PolyVoice::La`); the PPG is a Mono-voice model whose oscillators read wavetables; every table and attack is generated by our own code at start; and a DX7 SysEx file is parsed in Rust into the DX7's registry parameters.**

- **Where each runs.** `PolyVoice` (`poly.rs`) has `Fm(FmVoice)` for `Engine::Fm` and `La(LaVoice)` for `Engine::La`. There is no table variant. The PPG (`synth/ppgwave.rs`) is an `Engine::Mono` model with `uses_tables: true`: `TableOsc`s take the place of VCO 1 and 2 inside `MonoVoice`, through its ladder (`Filter::Ladder(PPG)`) and envelopes. The D-50's `LaVoice` reads only the attack samples (`SampleOsc`), never the wavetables.
- **The FM voice** (`fm.rs`, `fm/`): six sine operators with ratio or fixed frequency, coarse, fine and detune; a 4-rate/4-level envelope each; level scaling around a break point with four curves per side; rate scaling; velocity sensitivity. The DX7's 32 algorithms as routing flags per operator (`fm/algorithms.rs`), feedback on operator 6, the DX7's own LFO per voice, and a pitch envelope. Amplitude-modulation sensitivity is held but not applied, as in the reference. The DX7 model has 16 voices.
- **From the public format, msfa as reference.** `FmPatch` keeps the DX7's unpacked voice order (operator 6 first). The algorithm table, envelope and scaling curves follow Google's "music-synthesizer-for-android" (Apache-2.0). Dexed is not used. `NOTICE` records both.
- **SysEx in Rust** (`fm/sysex.rs`). `parse` reads a single voice (155 bytes), a 32-voice packed bank, or either without its framing. It is total: bad input is an `Error` (`Empty`, `NotYamaha`, `Unsupported`, `Truncated`), never a panic, and values are clamped as `FmPatch` clamps them. JavaScript sizes `sysex_buf` (at most `MAX_SYSEX`, 1 MiB) and writes the bytes; `load_sysex` parses and allocates in that call (ADR-0002's note).
- **A SysEx voice is a setting of parameters.** `apply_sysex` writes the voice's 6 × 21 operator fields to `Op1R1`… and its 19 globals to `PitchR1`… (`Algorithm`, `Feedback`, the LFO) through `set_param`, so the panel reads them back and the song saves them like any other value (ADR-0004). `SysexLoader.vue` forwards the file and the chosen index, and can keep the result as a user preset (ADR-0014).
- **Generated tables** (`table.rs`). `Tables::new` builds 8 wavetables (`Sweep`, `Pulse`, `Formant`, `Metal`, `Organ`, `Hollow`, `Digital`, `Bell`) of 64 waves of 256 samples, each wave summed from 31 harmonics off the sine table, and 8 attacks (`Chiff`, `Pluck`, `Bell`, `Marimba`, `Blow`, `Voice`, `Thump`, `Glass`) from seeded noise and decaying partials. A sample is an attack with an optional loop of its tail. `Tables::shared` builds them once per sample rate, in `Engine::new`. No ROM data is shipped.
- **The PPG** has two table oscillators with a position each (`Wt1Pos`, `Wt2Pos`), swept by the filter envelope (`EnvWt`) and the LFO (`LfoWt`). `WtSteps` jumps between waves instead of crossfading; `Character` puts samples on the 8-bit grid (`dac8`, #254). 8 voices.
- **The LA voice** (`la.rs`): two partials, each a synthesised saw or pulse (with PWM) or one of the attack samples (`Pcm1Sample`, `Pcm2Sample`), each through its own resonant ladder with its own TVF and TVA envelopes. `Structure` adds the pair, syncs partial 2 to 1 (both synthesised), or ring-modulates them. 16 voices.

## Consequences

- One `PolyVoice` enum holds every voice kind; the FM and LA voices are plain `Copy` data, so a model change rebuilds a pool without allocating (ADR-0011).
- The PPG gets the Mono voice's filters, envelopes, unison and analog variance for free, and user tables (ADR-0032, ADR-0033) play through the same path: `Wt1Table` 8–15 are user slots.
- Any DX7 voice or bank on the internet loads, and a loaded voice is just parameter values, editable, lockable per step and saved in the song.
- Tables are not band-limited: high notes alias, as the instruments did. Building them costs about 20 ms once per process.
- The sounds are ours, not the instruments'. A PPG wavetable or a D-50 PCM name here is a likeness, not a copy.
- Left out of the D-50: four-partial tones, dual and split tones (#88 asked for them; `la.rs` says so). Left out of the DX7: amplitude modulation sensitivity, and SysEx export from the view.

## Alternatives considered

- **Shipping ROM data** (the PPG's wavetables, the D-50's PCM, DX7 factory banks): authentic, but not ours to licence. Rejected; the user loads banks they own.
- **Porting Dexed**: a complete, faithful DX7, but GPL-3 code cannot enter an Apache-2.0 project. Rejected; msfa, Apache-2.0, is the reference instead.
- **A table voice of its own for the PPG**: a second copy of the Mono voice's filter and envelopes. Rejected; the PPG's sound is tables through an analog filter, which is the Mono voice.
- **The LA voice inside the Mono voice**: the D-50 needs a filter and two envelopes per partial and a ring or sync structure the Mono voice does not have. Rejected; a voice of its own.
- **Parsing SysEx in the view**: a second parser and a second idea of the DX7's parameters. Rejected (ADR-0001).
