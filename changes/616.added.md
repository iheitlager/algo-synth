- **Weighted random choice:** `%n` (1 to 16) after an option of `[a | b]` draws it n times as often as an option without one, as Strudel's and Tidal's `wchoose` and SuperCollider's `Pwrand`: `"c2 [c2%3 | eb2 | g1] ~ [[c3 bb2]%2 | g2]"` plays c2 as the second word three bars in five. A sequence takes its weight on its brackets; the draw is seeded, as `|` is. The Vim and tree-sitter syntaxes colour `|` and `%` as punctuation (#616).
