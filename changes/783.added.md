- **A hi-hat:** the `ModularHihat` preset, Moritz Klein's hi-hat (MKI x ES.EDU) as a SynthDef. Six square oscillators (261 to 123 Hz, moved together by the key, each at a fresh phase on every hit) go through a resonant band-pass, then a VCA whose envelope is its supply, so it clips as it gates. The key is TUNE (C4 plays the circuit as built) and the velocity the accent. Its panel: decay (closed to open), tone (the band from about 7 kHz down to about 4.5 kHz) and drive. `examples/sounds/hihat.song` plays a closed hat, an open hat and a ride (#783).
