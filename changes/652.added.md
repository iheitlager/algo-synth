- **General MIDI:** a `GeneralMidi` model, the multisampler on a program of the FluidR3 GM bank. Its twelve presets (`GmPiano`, `GmMarimba`, `GmAcousticBass`, `GmStrings`, …) each load their own pack, `gm-marimba` and so on, with no `samples` line; a written line or zone lines take its place. `make samples` reads the SoundFont (MIT) and builds the packs: one velocity layer, stereo pairs mixed to mono, each zone's attenuation kept. New example song `general-midi` (#652).
