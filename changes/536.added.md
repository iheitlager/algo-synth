- **A Roland TB-303:** one oscillator into a diode ladder, accent and slide as the 303 does them. An accented note (`!`) charges the accent sweep, so a run of accents opens the filter further each time, and a `&` slide glides on one gate. Three presets (`Tb303Acid`, `Tb303Squelch`, `Tb303Square`), its own panel, and `voicing: \tb303` on a Modular `MoogFF`. *Acid Workout* now plays on it (#536).
