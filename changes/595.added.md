- **Completion in the song editor:** as you type, a list of the words that fit where the caret is: keywords, track kinds, models and presets, methods and their arguments (clips and modes in `.scale(`, clips in `.chord(`), generator calls and arp modes, chord qualities and numerals, pads, `target.` parameters and the song's own clip, auto, snapshot and scene names, each with a line on what it is. The engine works them out on the main thread, so the audio never waits. Up/Down pick, Tab or Enter put the word in without applying the line, Esc closes, Ctrl+Space opens it (#595, #642).
