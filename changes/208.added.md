- **Modulation by signals:** `mod lead.cutoff = lfo(1).exprange(100, 2000) + lfo(3).range(0, 300)` moves any parameter while the song plays, in SuperCollider's and Strudel's terms: `sine saw tri square rand perlin lfo(rate, shape)`, `+ - * /`, `.range .exprange .slow .fast .segment .lag`. A signal follows the song's position, so a song renders the same every time; it is compiled at load and evaluated once a block, after lanes and scenes (ADR-0019, #208, #215).
- **Parameter names in any case:** `kit.cutoff` is `kit.Cutoff` in automation, scenes, settings and mixer lines (ADR-0019, #208).
