- **The Atari Punk Console, played like a synth:** the `ModularPunk` preset, Forrest Mims' Stepped Tone Generator (a 556) as a SynthDef. The key is the astable and the one-shot it triggers divides it into undertones, always in tune with the key. Its panel: width (the one-shot's pot, the mod wheel turns it on to the end), a track switch (1 the same steps on every key; 0 the real 0..55 ms, so higher keys divide further), a chip switch (0 the 556, its pulse never thinner than half the period; 1 transistors, thin pulses and rounded edges) and glide. `examples/sounds/atari-punk.song` plays it as a lead, chords and a stack (#781).
- **Assign Mono:** `Assign` gains Mono, one voice whatever the polyphony, beside Poly and Unison on every polyphonic synth, and the Modular's panel gets the Voices section (#781).
