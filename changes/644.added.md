- **How a call is written, as you type it:** inside a generator's, a pattern method's or a signal's parentheses the song editor shows its usage and an example (`prog takes a number of bars and a seed: prog(4,7)`), and a parameter method its range. The words come from one table that the parser's errors read too, so a mistake says the same; `every` and `off` now each name their own usage, and `.scale` and `.chord` errors give theirs (#644, #595).
- **`.struct(` in the song editor:** offers the earlier clips (#600) and shows its usage, the same line its error now gives (#644).
