- **`.shuffle(n)` and `.scramble(n)`:** each bar cut into n parts and played in a new order each bar, every part once (`shuffle`), or n parts drawn with repeats (`scramble`), as in Strudel and Tidal: `"0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7" .scale(a2 minor) .shuffle(4)`. Seeded, the same every run; the line repeats after 8 bars; they work inside `every`, `off` and `sometimes` and take a value a bar (#615).
