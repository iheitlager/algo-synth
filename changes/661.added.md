- **The `jazz` kit:** Strudel's `sound("jazz")`, the eight one-shots of Tidal's Dirt-Samples, fetched by `make samples` on their General MIDI pads and balanced by loudness: `samples kit jazz` on a Pad Sampler plays them from `bd`, `sn`, `ch` and `oh` lanes. Dirt-Samples has no license, so like the tidal-drum-machines kits it is for this machine only, never committed or published (#661).
