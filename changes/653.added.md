- **A drum clip made from a drum clip:** `clip stah = hatr hats .rev()` plays an earlier drum clip's steps (ghosts, flams and ratchets kept) through methods of time, back on its grid, so `.jux(rev)` works for drums too. A hit moved off the grid, a pitch method or a lane past 64 steps is an error; the completer offers drum clips and the methods of time there. The `jux` example's hats do it (#653).
