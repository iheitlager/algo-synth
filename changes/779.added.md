- **An FM drum:** the `ModularFmDrum` preset, Moritz Klein's FM drum (MKI x ES.EDU) as a SynthDef. The key is VCO1, the carrier, and the velocity the accent. Its panel: ratio (VCO2 against VCO1), fm (VCO2's exponential FM of VCO1), depth and pdecay (the pitch envelope on both), decay (the VCA), a wave switch (0 the diode-rounded sine, driven harder on the hit; 1 VCO1's pulse ring-modulated by VCO2 through the high-pass) and drive. `examples/sounds/fm-drum.song` tunes it into a kit: kick, tom, snare, hat and a metal bell (#779).
