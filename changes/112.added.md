- **Chips:** the C64's SID (6581/8580) and the NES's 2A03 as two models of a new Chips family, a chip per note at register precision: the SID's 16-bit frequency register, 12-bit waveforms, 8-bit noise, ADSR rate table (bug included), R-2R DACs and per-chip filter; the NES's 11-bit timers, duty cycles, 32-step triangle, LFSR noise, DMC delta samples from the sample store and non-linear mixer. Band-limited at the registers' steps or raw; a frame-rate player sweeps, arpeggiates and vibratos (#112).
- **Chip UGens:** `SidOsc`, `SidEnv`, `SidFilter`, `NesPulse`, `NesTri`, `NesNoise` and `NesDmc` in the Modular, and a `ModularSid` preset (#112).
- **NES drums:** with `NesKit` the NES plays a drum track's lanes from its channels, and any pad given a sample (a `samples` kit or `pad` line, as on the pad sampler) as 1-bit DMC deltas, as NES scores mixed them (#112).
- **Chip songs:** `sid-gauntlet` and `sid-shadows`, originals in the style of mid-80s C64 scores, and `nes-fortress`, of late-80s NES action scores (#112).
