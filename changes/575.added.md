- **Degree ranges and a scale octave:** `"0 .. 12"` (or `0..12`) plays every degree between, sharing the word's time, downward when the second is lower; the `scale` line takes an octave on its root, `scale c1 chromatic`, where a clip's degrees count from. Strudel's `n("0 .. 12").scale("C:chromatic")` is `scale c chromatic` over `"0 .. 12"` (#575).
