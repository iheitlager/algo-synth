- **The song text stays as typed:** it applies when a line is done (Enter, the caret leaving the line, or Ctrl+Enter), not when typing pauses, so a half-typed word never plays. An apply keeps your layout, spellings, blank lines and comments, and the words that play light up in them. A step click, the piano roll or a turned knob rewrites only the lines it changed, keeping their indent and comment. **Format** (Shift+Alt+F, or the composer's button) prints the whole song as the engine writes it (#588).
