- **The NJD dub siren:** `ModularDubSiren` gains rise (Ctl7, the climb when pressed), tone (Ctl8, the low-pass, down for the NJD clone's darker sound), a tail switch (Ctl9: 1 is the NJD clone's release, back to the base at full level while the square slows into ticks and stops) and a chip switch (Ctl10: 0 a 555, hard-edged with its duty moving with the sweep; 1 the NJD's transistor pair, a 50% square with rounded rising edges). `examples/sounds/dub-siren.song` has an `njd` setting and scene (#777).
